## FRAND licensing commitment

Primitive is willing to license, on fair, reasonable and non-discriminatory (FRAND) terms, the claims of the Primitive Patents essential to implementing the AFSP specification to any organization implementing AFSP in good faith and complying with the specification. The license terms, including whether any royalty applies, will be published before v1.0.

This commitment covers the technical mechanisms specified in AFSP. It does not extend to:

- access to the AFSP platform operated by Primitive;
- the AFSP certification program or certification mark, which are governed by the Network Participation Agreement;
- inclusion in the Participation Registry, which is governed by the Network Participation Agreement;
- the Return Trip API infrastructure operated by Primitive; or
- any analytics products or data services operated by Primitive.

Anyone may reproduce the AFSP specification without payment of royalties to Primitive, subject to attribution requirements.
