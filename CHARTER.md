# AFSP Technical Working Group Charter

## 1. Purpose

The AFSP Technical Working Group (the "Working Group") develops and reviews the AFSP specification and proposed extensions to it. AFSP v0.1 is governed by Primitive, as founding author and interim protocol administrator, until the AFSP Foundation is formed.

In this charter, the "AFSP Governing Body" means Primitive until governance transfers to the AFSP Foundation, and the AFSP Foundation after that transfer.

## 2. Scope

- Developing and versioning the AFSP specification
- Reviewing extension proposals
- The protocol binding, a reference implementation and conformance tests. This work starts with the working groups in November 2026, with first proofs of concept targeted for Q1 2027.

## 3. Membership

Participation is open to all organizations with a bona fide interest in the agentic banking channel. Each organization participates through named representatives and has one vote, however many representatives it has.

| Role | Who | Rights |
|---|---|---|
| **Founding Endorser** | Organizations that have submitted a written letter of endorsement and committed to participate in the Working Group, as listed in Appendix A | Standing participation; one vote |
| **Contributor** | Organizations admitted by the Working Group chair after asking to join by email to [afsp@primitive.com](mailto:afsp@primitive.com). Contributors must accept the AFSP contribution terms once they are published | Participate in discussion, drafting and review; one vote |
| **Observer** | Any organization that asks by email to [afsp@primitive.com](mailto:afsp@primitive.com) | Attend meetings and read materials; no vote |

Founding Endorsers and Contributors are the "voting members".

## 4. Decision-making

- The Working Group aims to decide by consensus. Consensus means that no voting member maintains an objection after discussion.
- If consensus cannot be reached, the chair may call a vote. A vote passes with a simple majority of the votes cast.
- Quorum for a vote is a majority of voting members.
- Type 2 (major) extensions require consensus, as Section 4.1 of the specification sets out.
- Working Group decisions on the specification are recommendations to the AFSP Governing Body, which approves them and publishes them as part of the specification.
- The AFSP Governing Body appoints the Working Group chair.

## 5. Extension review process

1. A proposal is submitted as a GitHub issue using the Extension proposal template, stating its extension type.
2. The Working Group triages new proposals within 10 business days.
3. Review status is tracked with labels (see CONTRIBUTING.md).
4. The Working Group records its decision and rationale on the issue.

Each extension type follows its own review path (Section 4.1 of the specification):

| Type | Review path |
|---|---|
| **Type 1: Minor** | Working group review; incorporated into the next point release |
| **Type 2: Major** | Working group consensus and a 60-day public comment period; incorporated into the next minor or major version |
| **Type 3: Profile** | Published as a companion document; does not modify the base specification |

## 6. Meetings

- The Working Group meets monthly by video conference, starting in November 2026. The chair may call additional meetings.
- The chair circulates the agenda to members at least five business days before each meeting.
- Minutes are shared with members within 10 business days of each meeting. Decisions on extension proposals are also recorded on the relevant GitHub issue.

## 7. Intellectual property

The specification is published under the terms in LICENSE and PATENTS.md, which follow Section 17.5 of the standard. Contributor terms will be published before contributions are accepted.

## 8. Transition to an independent foundation

Governance of AFSP will transfer to the AFSP Foundation, an independent non-profit, upon achievement of sufficient founding endorser participation. Formation is targeted for early 2027, with final timing set by the independent entity itself.

Primitive will work with the Founding Endorsers to form the AFSP Foundation. When governance transfers, the AFSP Foundation becomes the AFSP Governing Body. It takes over approving and publishing the specification and administering this charter, and the Working Group continues under the Foundation.

The Primitive Patents are not transferred to the AFSP Foundation and remain owned by CiDR Technologies, Inc. D/B/A Primitive. The FRAND licensing commitment in PATENTS.md continues to apply.

## 9. Amendments

This charter may be amended by a two-thirds vote of voting members, with the approval of the AFSP Governing Body. Proposed amendments are circulated to members at least 30 days before the vote.
